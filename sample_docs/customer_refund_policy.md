# Customer Refund Policy

## Standard Refund Window

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. The order number and purchase email are required for all refund requests.

## Non-Refundable Items

Opened software licenses, custom configuration services, and gift cards are non refundable. Shipping fees are not refundable unless the product arrived damaged or the wrong item was shipped.

## Process

Customers must submit refund requests through the support portal. The support team reviews requests within five to seven business days.

## Exceptions

If a product is defective, the customer may be eligible for replacement even after the 30 day refund window. The customer must provide photos or logs that show the defect.
