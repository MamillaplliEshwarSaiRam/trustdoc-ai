# Employee Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees are eligible for remote work after 60 days of employment if their role can be performed securely outside the office. Remote work is allowed up to three days per week with manager approval.

## Approval

Employees must submit a remote work request at least five business days before the first remote work day. The request must include working hours, contact information, and confirmation that company data will be accessed only from approved devices.

## Security

Company data must not be stored on personal devices. Employees are required to use multi-factor authentication and the company VPN when accessing internal systems.

## Exceptions

Remote work may not be allowed for roles that require physical access to secure equipment. Managers may revoke remote work approval if performance, availability, or security requirements are not met.
